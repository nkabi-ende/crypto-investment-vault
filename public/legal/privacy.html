Privacy Notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

CIV uses account registration information and authentication cookies to provide secure access. This notice describes the website's present data-handling approach and should be reviewed against operational practices before a public launch.

1. Information processed

Registration names and email addresses, password hashes, session identifiers, referral attribution, associated wallet addresses, verified transaction hashes, funding records, promotional credit status, security events and technical request information may be processed to operate and protect the website. Passwords are not stored in plaintext. Public blockchain transactions may be visible to others and cannot generally be erased from the blockchain.

2. Purposes and lawful grounds

Information is used to create accounts, authenticate users, maintain service security, investigate misuse and comply with applicable legal duties, subject to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

3. Service providers and transfers

Cloudflare provides hosting and database infrastructure. Third-party market-data and chart providers may receive technical requests when their services are used. Cross-border processing should be assessed and safeguarded as required by law.

4. Cookies

Necessary session cookies are used for account authentication. Any non-essential analytics or advertising technologies should be separately disclosed and managed according to applicable consent requirements.

5. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained, with retention limited to legitimate purposes and legal requirements. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Subject to POPIA, individuals may request access, correction or deletion where applicable, object to certain processing and complain to South Africa's Information Regulator.

7. Contact

For access, correction, objection, deletion and other privacy requests, email info@cryptoinvestmentvault.com. Technical security concerns can be sent to dev@cryptoinvestmentvault.com. The verified legal operator and designated information-officer details must still be disclosed as required by law before regulated services launch.

CIV Circle public partner information

When your first referral invitation is accepted, CIV automatically creates a public partner alias separate from your name, email, member ID and invitation code. Affiliate participation includes public leaderboard inclusion; there is no optional visibility switch. The board shows the alias, current tier, qualified direct referral count and broad capital-introduced bands. Capital bands are shown only after at least five qualifying referrals. Customer names, emails, wallet addresses and individual balances are never included in this public response. Private partner reporting contains commission statuses and aggregate referral progress. Partner aliases do not imply custody or management of referred customers' assets.

A first-party, HttpOnly invitation cookie and an expiring server-side referral claim help preserve attribution for up to 30 days before registration. Your inviter is bound to your account at registration, so subsequent MiniPay or browser steps do not depend on that cookie. An invitation code does not grant account access.

Identity verification documents

When you choose identity verification, CIV stores your ID photograph, proof of address, document category, size, fingerprint and upload time, linked to your authenticated account ID. Files are held privately in the account database; they are not public website assets. Authorised reviewers can access submitted evidence to check identity, address and document validity. Access, submission revisions, reviewer decisions, notes and verification expiry are recorded.

Document receipt is followed by review. An approved, unexpired identity record enables withdrawal requests; a separate payment review applies before any payout. Registration and account-specific deposit eligibility do not require CIV identity verification. Verification status may be shown beside your name inside your private dashboard; document contents are not included in public profile or leaderboard responses.

Documents and review history are retained for verification, fraud prevention, dispute handling and applicable retention duties. Contact info@cryptoinvestmentvault.com for access, correction, retention or deletion enquiries. Requests are reviewed against continuing operational and legal obligations. No biometric face matching or third-party automated identity decision is performed by this document-upload workflow.

Account data and invitations

When you create an account, CIV stores your name, email, password digest, consent version and time, session records, and account activity. If you use an invitation link, its code is stored with your registration so that registrations can be counted. The inviter can see a count, not your name or email. Your browser may retain an invitation code for up to 30 days.

A private transactional email queue records a welcome-message event. Messages are sent only after a delivery provider has been connected. Creating an account does not verify ownership of your email address. Referral rewards are tracked as conditional promotional ZAR credits when an invited member completes a qualifying verified production stablecoin deposit. Signup or testnet activity alone does not qualify; credits are not currently withdrawable.
