Common questions

Know before you decide.

Do I need MiniPay to create a CIV account?

No. You can join and use the market dashboard without MiniPay. When deposit access is available to your account, MiniPay is the supported payment wallet for USDC or USDT on Celo. See the three-step setup.

How do I buy USDC or USDT to deposit?

Download MiniPay on your phone and create a wallet. Inside MiniPay choose Add Cash and a funding provider available in your region. Once USDC or USDT arrives, return to your CIV dashboard for a separate, account-specific payment request and approve it in MiniPay. Buying stablecoins does not fund CIV automatically. Deposit availability is shown in your dashboard.

Does The Big 7 guarantee daily or weekly returns?

No. Crypto markets are volatile and leveraged trading can produce substantial losses. Any future distributions would depend on actual results and disclosed terms.

Where will investor funds be held?

Custody arrangements have not been published. CIV must disclose the custodian, legal terms, segregation model and withdrawal process before funding is enabled.

Are the prices live?

CIV requests indicative USD spot quotes from CoinGecko through its server-side API, with cached snapshots refreshed roughly every ten minutes. TradingView charts display separate exchange USDT trading pairs. Provider outages and delays can occur.

How do deposits and the R500 promo work?

The minimum is 100 USDC or USDT, with 200 as the next deposit option. A verified mainnet deposit activates membership. R500 is a separate promotion, pending final offer terms; it is not a signup fee or withdrawable deposit balance. CIV is a platform, not a token.

How will I assess performance?

Before funding opens, CIV should provide verifiable historical returns net of fees, maximum drawdown, trading exposure, independent verification and a clear methodology. These records are not yet available.

Interactive / Exposure mechanics

Understand leverage before exposure.

Leverage increases market exposure relative to collateral. It magnifies losses as quickly as gains. These calculations are simplified and exclude funding costs, fees, maintenance margin and exchange-specific liquidation rules.

Select leverage

Example uses 100 USDT collateral and assumes a long position. Short positions reverse the direction of gains and losses.
