Research / Asset intelligence

Know what sits inside The Big 7.

Seven different crypto assets, each with its own market drivers and risk profile. These descriptions are educational, not investment recommendations.

Bitcoin
BTC
Store-of-value / benchmark

The largest and most established crypto asset by market recognition. Bitcoin often anchors market sentiment; its price remains highly volatile.

Ethereum
ETH
Smart-contract infrastructure

The Ethereum network supports decentralized applications and smart contracts. Ether exposure includes network adoption, competition and market risks.

Solana
SOL
High-throughput blockchain

Solana focuses on fast, lower-cost transactions and a broad application ecosystem. Network reliability and ecosystem concentration are relevant risks.

XRP
XRP
Payments-oriented asset

XRP is a digital asset associated with the XRP Ledger and payments use cases. Regulatory developments and adoption can materially affect its price.

BNB
BNB
Exchange ecosystem / network token

BNB is used within the BNB Chain ecosystem and related services. Platform concentration and regulatory risk warrant close monitoring.

Cardano
ADA
Proof-of-stake blockchain

ADA is Cardano's native asset, used within a proof-of-stake smart-contract network. Development and ecosystem adoption are key uncertainties.

Dogecoin
DOGE
Community-driven crypto asset

Dogecoin is a widely recognized cryptocurrency with significant sentiment-driven price moves. Speculative volatility can be extreme.

Interactive / Exposure mechanics

Understand leverage before exposure.

Leverage increases market exposure relative to collateral. It magnifies losses as quickly as gains. These calculations are simplified and exclude funding costs, fees, maintenance margin and exchange-specific liquidation rules.

Select leverage

Example uses 100 USDT collateral and assumes a long position. Short positions reverse the direction of gains and losses.

Important investment risk

Leveraged crypto trading is highly speculative and can result in rapid or total loss of capital. Returns are not guaranteed, including the previously proposed 50% daily scenario. CIV does not currently accept investment deposits or execute trades.

Common questions

Know before you decide.

Can I deposit USDT today?

No. Deposits and withdrawals are disabled. Account registration is for accessing the platform and market information.

Does The Big 7 guarantee daily or weekly returns?

No. Crypto markets are volatile and leveraged trading can produce substantial losses. Any future distributions would depend on actual results and disclosed terms.

Where will investor funds be held?

Custody arrangements have not been published. CIV must disclose the custodian, legal terms, segregation model and withdrawal process before funding is enabled.

Are the prices live?

CIV requests indicative USD spot quotes from CoinGecko through its server-side API, refreshed at most every five minutes. TradingView charts display separate exchange USDT trading pairs. Provider outages and delays can occur.

What does 200 USDT mean?

It is a proposed entry reference, not an active minimum deposit or payment instruction. USDT is not identical to USD, and any ZAR equivalent depends on current market exchange rates.

How will I assess performance?

Before funding opens, CIV should provide verifiable historical returns net of fees, maximum drawdown, trading exposure, independent verification and a clear methodology. These records are not yet available.
